## Perception: Berkeley and Merleau-Ponty

- [Berkeley: *Principles of Human Knowledge*, section 6](https://celt.ucc.ie/published/E710001-001/text005.html): a short primary-source passage for the camera-disconnected robot question.
- [Merleau-Ponty: *Phenomenology of Perception*, original French preface](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/merleau_ponty_maurice/phonomenologie_de_la_perception/phonomenologie_de_la_perception_avant_propos.html): look for "La perception n'est pas une science du monde".
- [Routledge introduction to *Phenomenology of Perception*](https://www.routledge.com/Phenomenology-of-Perception/Merleau-Ponty/p/book/9780415834339): short introductory context.
- [NotebookLM: AI Perception](https://notebooklm.google.com/notebook/af5ed21e-57cd-4d0a-b2a1-8c96e3e75d74): ask for context and source locations, then inspect the actual passage.
- [NotebookLM podcast](https://notebooklm.google.com/notebook/af5ed21e-57cd-4d0a-b2a1-8c96e3e75d74?artifactId=4f0253fe-b5c6-4697-8177-92c4323405b0): listen to an overview later.
