# Session 02: Tools and Useful Resources

**Use this only when it helps your project.** There is no required tool list for the Open Project Studio.

## For the shared experiment

- [Replicate Explore](https://replicate.com/explore): possible image-generation playground. Access will be explained in class.
- Any available AI writing assistant: try generating and then editing the fictional artist's statement. The point is how you shape and frame the result, not the brand of model.

## For your own project

Use what supports your project: photography, drawing, sound recording, video, text, editing software, image generation, interface design, coding, or another medium.

Ask yourself: **What is the AI-assisted element? What do I make without AI? What do I want a viewer to experience?**

Tools can create outputs, but the choice of system, the selection, framing, editing, and your original human contribution are part of the process. Document major choices for your later reflection. Follow the official brief for the assignment requirements.
